## 4. Responsibilities of the Processor

The Processor shall:

4.1 **Data Minimization**
- Process only the data categories strictly necessary for the purpose
- Not use data for any other purpose without written consent from the Controller

4.2 **Security**
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:
  - Encryption of data at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+, AES-256)
  - Role-based access control (RBAC)
  - Regular security audits and penetration testing
  - Incident response procedures
  - Staff training on data protection

4.3 **Sub-processors**
- Not engage sub-processors without prior written authorization from the Controller
- Ensure sub-processors are bound by equivalent data protection obligations
- Maintain a current list of authorized sub-processors

4.4 **Data Subject Rights**
- Assist the Controller in fulfilling data subject requests (access, deletion, portability)
- Respond to requests within 10 business days

4.5 **Audit & Inspection**
- Allow the Controller and/or its DPO to audit processing activities
- Provide auditing certificates (ISO 27001, SOC 2) upon request
- Maintain documentation of processing activities

4.6 **Confidentiality**
- Ensure all staff with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality
- Disclose data only to authorized individuals for the defined purpose

## 5. Responsibilities of the Controller

The Controller shall:

5.1 **Authorization**
- Ensure processing is lawful under applicable law (GDPR, Schulgesetz)
- Obtain parental/student consent where required (Art. 8 GDPR for minors)

5.2 **Instructions**
- Provide written instructions for all processing activities
- Prohibit any processing outside the defined scope

5.3 **Data Quality**
- Ensure data is accurate and up-to-date
- Obtain informed consent from data subjects (or parents for minors)

---

## 6. Data Breach Notification

6.1 **Processor Notification**
If the Processor discovers a data breach or suspected unauthorized processing, it shall notify the Controller within **24 hours** with:
- Description of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of affected individuals
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach

6.2 **Controller's Reporting Duty**
The Controller is responsible for notifying the relevant supervisory authority (Landesbeauftragte) and affected individuals, as required by GDPR Art. 33-34.

---

## 7. Data Deletion & Return

Upon termination of this agreement, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice:

- Delete all personal data and existing copies (unless retention is required by law)
- Return all personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format

Deletion shall be completed within **30 days** of termination and certified in writing.

---

## 8. Data Transfer & Location

8.1 **Processing Location**
Data shall be processed exclusively within the **European Union/EEA**.

8.2 **International Transfers**
Any transfer of data outside the EU/EEA requires:
- Prior written consent from the Controller
- Compliance with GDPR Chapter 5 (adequacy decisions or appropriate safeguards)
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) if required

## 9. Term & Termination

9.1 **Term**
This agreement remains effective for the duration of the service contract.

9.2 **Termination**
Either party may terminate with **30 days written notice** if the other party materially breaches this agreement and fails to remedy within 15 days.

9.3 **Data Handling Upon Termination**
Upon termination, the Processor shall delete or return all personal data as directed by the Controller (Section 7).

## 10. Liability

The Processor shall indemnify the Controller for:
- Breaches of this agreement or applicable data protection law
- Unauthorized processing or disclosure of data
- Failure to implement required security measures

Liability is limited to direct damages and shall not exceed annual fees under the service contract.
